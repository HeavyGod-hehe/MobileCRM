Help Guide

Where to record money — one simple rule for each situation

Daily cash in your drawer

Use Cash Book. Every cash sale, cash expense, or petty cash goes here.

  • Food / tea / shop expense → Cash Book → Out + pick category (Food, Entertainment, etc.)
  • Customer paid cash for a phone → Cash Book → In
  • End of day cash count → check balance on Cash Book page

Person owes you / you owe a person (Udhar / Khata)

Use Accounts. One account per person or supplier.

  • Customer bought on credit → Accounts → their name → Debit (udhar given)
  • They paid back → Accounts → Payment (credit)
  • Send balance on WhatsApp → open their ledger → WhatsApp Balance

Bank deposit / withdraw / transfer

Use Accounts → Bank Accounts section.

  • Deposit cash to bank → Bank account → Credit
  • Withdraw from ATM → Bank account → Debit

Software updates

Open Settings → Software Update to check for a new version. In the compiled desktop app you can install updates in one click — your database in Data/ is not touched.
